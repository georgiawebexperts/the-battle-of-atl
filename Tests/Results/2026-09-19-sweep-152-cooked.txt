## The Ghost Rider

`BattleFinishAudit` is Windows' gate on the feature they shipped in round 15 -
five wins on a difficulty, a recorded best route, a ghost that rides it on the
real clock, and a beaten flag when you beat your best while it rides. It passes
here from all three binaries, which is the convergence they asked for when they
pushed `4cc3a7f`:

  editor (the extended run):  -- BattleFinishAudit: pass
  the staged cooked build:    -- BattleFinishAudit: pass
  the assembled share:        -- BattleFinishAudit: pass

The share is the artifact the Desktop shortcut runs, so this is the packaged
proof rather than an editor one.

## How much of the coverage was never run

75 audit entry points exist in `BattleMacController.cpp`; the standard sweep ran
31 of them. The other 44 had never been run by anything, and "outside the sweep"
is exactly how this project's own notes say BattleSkaterAudit, BattleDroneAudit
and BattleFrisbeeAudit stayed red unnoticed. Two of the 44 are gates on features
shipped the same day: `BattleFinishAudit` (the ghost) and `BattleInventoryAudit`
(the weapon models behind the "the gun should change" complaint).

They are now runnable without slowing the standard sweep:

  BATTLE_SWEEP_EXTENDED=1 zsh Scripts/run_quick_audits.sh <binary>

with a two-minute cap each rather than seven.
