# Trouble and police — build 032

2026-09-11 [codex-maclaptop]

A direct bike collision above 500 cm/s with a pedestrian reports an assault through the bike impact callback. A damaging pedestrian shot reports the same event. Three distinct people hit in one run unlock police response; repeated contact with the same actor does not increase that count. The alert persists for the run. Up to two live officers are allowed nearby, with twelve-second spawn checks (a new civilian assault can request an earlier check) and reachable, dry navigation placement at least eleven metres from the player.

Each actual gun/launcher shot raises danger by 0.75, each new civilian assault by three, capped at twelve. After twenty quiet seconds danger decays by 0.15 per second. High danger can bring an occasional officer even before the three-person alert. Danger adds up to six nearby zombies and shortens their respawn interval. Artifact possession adds another six and increases spawning frequency. These are initial balance values; live playtest acceptance is outstanding.

An officer pursues on navigation, warns for 1.25 seconds within eight metres with clear line of sight, and fires a visible taser line. Breaking line of sight cancels the warning. A hit subtracts ten seconds and stuns for three seconds, attempts a safe dismount and lowers the foot camera during recovery. Blocked dismount locations are retried. The rider must remount after recovery; there is no arrest, checkpoint teleport or taser health death. Twelve seconds of taser grace and a fifteen-second officer cooldown prevent chained stuns. Ordinary attackers can still cause health damage. A damaging player shot on a police officer costs sixty seconds and awards no zombie time or kill nitro.

The officer now uses the full rigged Quaternius Swat character with an authored navy/black/visor palette; source and CC0 license are recorded in SourceAssets/Rider/SOURCE.md. The primitive uniform was rejected during visual review and removed. This is still stylized art, not final GTA-quality character art, ragdoll or a completed crash-animation system. The taser recovery camera and immobilization are implemented; full authored fall/get-up animation remains unfinished.

The native audit isolates unrelated crowds/zombies. It exercises an injected bike impact callback, distinct-person tracking, a real navigation police spawn and measured pursuit, actual player gunfire and police damage/time penalty, Artifact population changes, a physical blocking wall, real officer windup/taser hit, dismount, action lock, grace, remount and clearing stun on checkpoint respawn after a health death. The synthetic collision callback is not proof of a complete physical riding playtest. Native rendered captures are separate controlled fixtures, not evidence of live combat balance.
