# Mac playtest 048

2026-09-12 [codex-maclaptop]

Candidate version: 0.48.0. Packaging in progress; desktop remains 047 until installation and verification finish.

This build adds Monroe traffic and signals, yielding traffic at the Irwin/Lake BeltLine crossing, a first pass at nearby buildings and sidewalks, and one shallow pothole near Irwin. The buildings use generic facades and estimated heights; this is not a finished recreation of the real businesses.

Review while riding:

- Approach Monroe in both directions. Vehicles must respect crossing occupancy and avoid clipping through road joins.
- Continue to Irwin/Lake. Watch for vehicles yielding where the trail crosses the road. No invented traffic-light cycle is used here.
- Ride past the shallow pothole and then over it. A clear pass should not trigger an impact; a direct hit should jolt and scrub speed once, without a shallow-hole wipeout.
- Dismount near the Irwin buildings and approach the doors. Doors remain closed; the exterior landings should not trap the player.
- Check combined traffic performance and readability while moving. Separate review scenes do not establish performance of the full world.

Required packaged checks before installing: cook completion, stationary-car recovery, moving-car recovery, shared health/checkpoint reset, strict local code-signature validation. Packaged visual review and the full gameplay requirements remain outstanding regardless of those narrow checks.

Known limitations: existing crash animations, generic streetscape, additional intersections including Krog/DeKalb, traffic variety, deep potholes and many original full-game requirements remain unfinished. This is a development playtest, not a final release.
