# Chain-lock melee

2026-09-11 [codex-maclaptop]

F swings a U-lock on a chain while on foot. The swing takes 0.7 seconds, resolves
once after a 0.22-second windup, and prevents firing or starting a second swing.
Reloading prevents melee; melee prevents starting a reload through R. Swimming,
death, a paused game, the countdown, and an ended run prevent attacks.

The strike sweeps a 28 cm sphere 185 cm forward from below the camera. The first
blocking visibility surface stops it, including walls. The player's parked bike
is ignored. A hit deals 50 ordinary damage without the pistol headshot multiplier.
Zombies flinch, bleed and, if alive, have their attack interrupted and receive a
short knockback. Two hits kill a full-health zombie and award the existing kill
statistic and nitro reward once. A miss still consumes the swing's recovery time.

The visible model is built from steel rods and interlocking links, attached to
the first-person camera. Its swing moves across the view and the right arm follows
the chain grip; the pistol is hidden until recovery. Original synthesized chain
swing and impact effects are generated by Scripts/generate_melee_audio.py and
imported with Scripts/prepare_melee_assets.py. These are procedural prototype
art and sounds; their appearance and feel need a rendered review.

Scripts/test_mac_melee.py runs a cooked native game with the opt-in
BattleMeleeAudit flag. It sends F press/release events through the controller,
checks windup, repeated-swing and fire exclusion, real zombie damage and reward,
then wall and distance fixtures plus reload/end/death guards. Targets are placed
and frozen to isolate collision rules. It does not verify physical keyboard
capture, human aim, animation quality, audio quality or frame rate.

Shotgun, SMG, ricocheting frisbee launcher, weapon inventory, crates and enemy
weapon drops remain required by V3. This melee implementation does not complete
the broader weapon system.
