# Rare knife encounters

2026-09-11 [codex-maclaptop]

The first landed stab forces a nonfatal dismount. After a short recovery, Ellison can run, draw his weapon or remount. A second landed stab from the same attacker kills him and invokes the existing phone-hunt reset. Successful remount ends that attacker's chase immediately. Windup lasts 0.85 seconds and reach/line of sight are checked again at impact; getting out of reach or behind a wall can make the attack miss. Recovery protection prevents stacked hits.

The spawner checks every 90–150 seconds, permits one living attacker, and chooses reachable dry ground outside direct view. Baseline chance is 3% before collecting the phone, 15% afterward, plus up to 10 percentage points from trouble. Tutorial, countdown, swimming and ended runs exclude encounters. These are initial tuning values, not playtest-approved balance.

Knife attackers count as hostiles, not zombies or civilians. Defeating one gives no zombie time reward. Gunfire still increases trouble. The current generic character, procedural knife motion and tipping death animation are placeholders requiring a dedicated visual pass.

Candidate cooked acceptance passed on Easy and Hard: actual navigation to the player, first-hit hands-free dismount, recovery/remount escape, two-hit death/reset, wall obstruction, incomplete windup, tutorial protection, defeated-attacker cancellation and no zombie time award. On-foot regression also passed. These tests do not verify visual quality, actual player gun aiming against the attacker, long-run spawn balance or the full rare-spawn path. Candidate remains staged, not installed as build 044; installed build 043 remains the playable version.
