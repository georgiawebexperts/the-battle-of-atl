THE BATTLE OF ATL — MAC PLAYTEST 059
Early, unfinished playtest • September 13, 2026

WHAT YOU NEED
An Apple silicon Mac (M1 or newer). This build targets macOS 14 or later.
Intel Macs, Windows, iPhones and iPads cannot run this build.
Allow at least 8 GB free for downloading, extracting and copying the app.
Performance on other Macs has not yet been established.
You do NOT need Unreal Engine, Aura, Xcode or an Apple Developer account.
A mouse and keyboard are recommended.

INSTALL AND OPEN
1. Download the ZIP sent by Elliott. Double-click it to extract the folder.
2. Drag “The Battle of ATL.app” into your Applications folder.
   Copy the complete app, not an alias or shortcut. Keep these instructions.
3. In Applications, double-click “The Battle of ATL”.
4. macOS may block it because this private test is not signed with an Apple
   Developer ID and has not been notarized by Apple. This is expected.
   Wording varies: “developer cannot be verified” or “Apple could not verify
   ... is free of malware” are examples. Choose Done/Cancel if offered.
5. If you trust the copy received directly from Elliott, open Apple menu >
   System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security.
6. Find the message about this app and click Open Anyway, if offered.
7. Authenticate with your Mac password or Touch ID, then click Open in the
   confirmation. You should normally be able to open it normally afterward.
   If Open Anyway is missing, attempt to open the app again and return here.

Do not disable Gatekeeper, SIP, or other system-wide security settings.
No Terminal commands are required by this guide. A work/school Mac may have
management rules that prevent the exception; ask its administrator.

If the message specifically says the app “will damage your computer”, or
says it is damaged, STOP and send Elliott a screenshot. Do not treat every
warning as the expected unidentified-developer prompt. Re-download if the
ZIP was incomplete; do not repeatedly override a different warning.

Apple's official instructions:
https://support.apple.com/102445

FIRST RUN
Choose START PARK RIDE. The home/practice area lets you learn before the
park timer begins. The first load may take longer than later loads.
If the game looks frozen, give it a little time; do not launch extra copies.
Click inside the game window to focus it. Press Escape for the menu.

THE OBJECTIVE
You are Ellison. Find the lost phone using the watch's general direction,
then follow the route toward Krog Street Tunnel and Morgan at 98 Estoria.
This is a fictional, unfinished game, not a real-world navigation guide.
Contains fictional violence and blood.

BIKE CONTROLS
W / Up arrow             Pedal
A / D or Left / Right    Steer
S / Down arrow or Space  Brake
Q / R                    Lower / higher gear (five gears)
J                        Jump while moving
Left Shift               Boost
H                        Horn (limited uses)
E                        Get off the bike
P                        Switch arcade / realistic bike handling
Tab                      Change bike camera

ON FOOT
Mouse                    Turn camera
WASD / arrow keys        Move relative to camera
Left Shift               Run
Space                    Jump
C / Left Control         Crouch
G                        Draw / holster weapon
Right mouse button       Aim while weapon is drawn
Left mouse button        Fire while weapon is drawn
R                        Reload using collected ammunition
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5        Select acquired pistol / shotgun / SMG / frisbee / rifle
F                        Melee
E near your bike         Get back on (after recovery; not while swimming)
Escape                   Pause/menu

You start with 17 pistol rounds. Pistol ammo pickups give 17 rounds to
reserve; reload to use them. The timer drains faster while on foot.
Close the game window or use Command-Q to quit.

KNOWN ISSUES — PLEASE EXPECT ROUGH EDGES
This is not a finished release. Models, environment, animation, hand/weapon
alignment and balance still need work. Police pursuit can be inconsistent;
police voice and the complete visible taser sequence are not finished.
Ammo placement near trash cans is not finished. Nearby ammo markers are new.
Mouse sensitivity was reduced again for this build and still needs feedback.
Real-world hill/ramp riding, camera transitions and performance need testing.
The app is locally signed only; Apple has not notarized or reviewed it.
